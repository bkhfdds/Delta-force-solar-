Our installation process

From first call to a working system.

  1. Consultation

    We talk through your power needs, your property and your goals.

  2. Site assessment and design

    We look at your roof or site and design a system to fit.

  3. Quotation

    You receive a clear proposal before any work begins.

  4. Installation

    Panels, mounting, inverter and wiring are fitted and connected.

  5. Testing and handover

    We test the system and show you how it works.
